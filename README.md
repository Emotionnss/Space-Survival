# Space Survival

> A sci-fi bullet-hell survival game where you must dodge enemy attacks, shoot down incoming ships, and escape before time runs out.

## About the Game

**Space Survival** is a mobile sci-fi arcade game inspired by bullet-hell games such as *Touhou*.

Players take control of a spaceship and must survive an enemy attack by dodging incoming bullets while shooting down enemy ships.

With a one-minute time limit and limited health, players must balance their movement and shooting to defeat enough enemies before making their escape.

## Story

You are the last surviving royalty of the Korgol Kingdom.

After a devastating war, your family and friends were lost, and your kingdom was destroyed by the Meracanies.

With everything you once had taken away, your only choice is to escape to another planet and rebuild your empire.

However, the enemy forces are still pursuing you. Before you can activate your hyperdrive and escape, you must fight your way through their incoming ships and survive the attack.

## Main Objective

Survive the enemy assault by shooting down the required number of enemy ships before the one-minute time limit expires.

Players must avoid incoming attacks, manage their movement, and defeat enemies to secure their escape.

## Gameplay

### 1. Player Controls

The game uses simple touch-based controls designed for mobile devices.

- **Touch:** Move the spaceship towards the selected position.
- **Drag:** Continuously move the spaceship by following your finger.
- **Hold:** Move the spaceship while automatically firing projectiles upwards.

The player does not need a separate shooting button, allowing them to focus on movement and avoiding enemy attacks.

### 2. Player Attacks

The player's spaceship automatically fires small blue beams called **P_Bullets** when the screen is held.

- P_Bullets travel upwards in a single direction.
- Each successful hit reduces the enemy's health.
- Players must position themselves carefully to hit moving enemies.

### 3. Enemy Movement & Attacks

Enemies have different movement patterns that change unpredictably, making them more difficult to target.

They also retaliate by firing yellow beams called **E_Bullets** towards the player.

**Enemy Bullet Characteristics:**
- E_Bullets are aimed at the player's position when they are fired.
- Once fired, their direction remains fixed.
- Bullets continue travelling along their original trajectory.

Players must constantly reposition themselves to avoid incoming attacks.

### 4. Combat & Survival

Combat requires players to balance attacking and dodging.

While shooting enemies is relatively straightforward, successfully defeating them requires good positioning and awareness.

Players must:
- Dodge incoming enemy bullets.
- Avoid direct contact with enemies.
- Continuously shoot at enemy ships.
- Defeat the required number of enemies before time runs out.

### 5. Enemy Defeats & Kill Count

Successfully defeating an enemy increases the player's kill count.

The player must reach the required number of kills to complete the level and escape.

## Winning & Losing Conditions

### Winning the Game

The player wins by defeating the required number of enemies within the one-minute time limit.

Upon winning:
- The game transitions to the ending screen.
- The player's completion time is recorded and displayed.

### Losing the Game

The player loses if either of the following conditions occurs:

- **Time Limit:** The player fails to defeat the required number of enemies within one minute.
- **Health Limit:** The player gets hit by enemy bullets three times.

If either condition is met, the player loses and is taken to the Game Over screen.

## Game Story & Objective

### Story Direction

The game follows the last surviving member of the Korgol royal family as they attempt to escape the Meracanian forces.

The player's immediate objective is to survive the enemy attack and escape using the hyperdrive.

The larger story revolves around rebuilding the fallen Korgol Kingdom and reclaiming what was lost.

### Project Objective

I have always wanted to experiment with creating a sci-fi game and exploring the bullet-hell genre.

By combining both ideas, I wanted to create a simple but challenging arcade experience with a small amount of storytelling and action.

The goal was to make a game that is easy to understand and play, but still requires practice and skill to master.

---

## Inspiration & References

### Touhou

The game takes inspiration from **Touhou**, a well-known bullet-hell game series recognised for its challenging gameplay and complex bullet patterns.

Inspired by its combat style, Space Survival takes a simpler and more approachable approach to bullet-hell gameplay.

Rather than overwhelming players with extremely difficult patterns, the game focuses on readable enemy attacks, straightforward controls, and manageable challenges while still requiring good timing and movement.
